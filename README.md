## Open locally

Unzip the package, then double-click `index.html`. Keep `resume.pdf` and `targets.csv` beside it so those download links work. The styling, scripts, Chan’s portrait and restaurant image are embedded in the HTML; no installation, build step or internet connection is needed to use the presentation. External source and LinkedIn links require internet.

- `index.html`: 14-section interactive presentation, market framework, account search and tier filters, unit-economics calculator, weekly plan, B2B landing-page mockup, Why Chan and credentials.

- `resume.pdf`: supplied résumé, unchanged.
- `.nojekyll`: static GitHub Pages configuration.

## GitHub Pages

1. Create or choose a GitHub repository.
2. Upload the **contents** of this folder, including `index.html`, to the repository root on your publishing branch (usually `main`). Do not upload only the ZIP.
3. Open repository **Settings → Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Select your branch and **/(root)**, then save.
6. When deployment finishes, open the URL shown in GitHub Pages settings.

Official instructions: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/configuring-a-publishing-source-for-your-github-pages-site

## Interpretation

All prices, volumes, conversion targets and financial outputs are planning hypotheses in CAD before tax, not validated SHUSHU results or market averages. The Market Opportunity section uses a five-customer weekly sales example, not an estimate of the total Montréal market. The shortlist is not 50 verified cafeterias. Local employee counts, operators, attendance, routes and contract access require qualification; known research gaps remain visible.

The 2026 Montréal employer directory supports local presence for most prospects. Cafeteria evidence is linked for Air Canada and National Bank; CN evidence is historical (2016). Tiers are outreach proposals, not numeric evidence-based fit rankings.

The tasting form is a local presentation demo: it does not transmit or store entries. The calculator uses 48 operating weeks / 12 months and treats delivery as a cost per account service. Inputs reset on reload. No analytics, trackers or external scripts are included.
